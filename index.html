    01Philosophy

    We do not clean cars. We restore the conditions in which a surface tells the truth.

    1. I

      Restraint

      We add nothing the car does not already deserve. We remove what hides it.

    2. II

      Precision

      Every decision is made against the surface in front of us, never against the clock.

    3. III

      Permanence

      A finish is judged a year from now, not under a showroom lamp.

    02Craftsmanship

    Craftsmanship: the lamp, the line, the wheel

    i / iii

    The lamp

    Lenses haze and micro-scratch long before they fail. Restored, they give back the light the car was designed to throw.

    ii / iii

    The line

    Paint is read as reflection. A true panel carries one unbroken line of light; swirls and haze break it into noise.

    iii / iii

    The wheel

    Barrel, face and caliper take the harshest contamination on the car. Cleaned and sealed, they are easier to keep that way.

    03Surface lab

    Five ways we work the surface.

    Paint correction

    Swirl marks, haze and fine scratches live in the clear coat and scatter light. Correction levels the surface so a panel reflects as one clean image again.

    Illustration: both halves are the same 3D model. The “before” marks are generated to show the kind of defect correction addresses. It is not a photograph or a measured result.

    Paint protection film

    A transparent urethane film applied over paint to take stone chips and abrasion that would otherwise reach the clear coat. Coverage is chosen to suit the vehicle and how it is used.

    Illustration: the highlighted band marks an area in the model’s own coordinates, not individual panels. Real coverage is agreed per vehicle.

    Ceramic coating

    A thin, hard layer bonded to prepared paint. It does not change the shape of a panel; it changes how the surface behaves day to day. Preparation decides how good it looks.

    This light is how any finish is read: the reflection should travel the panel unbroken. It is an inspection aid, not a before/after of a coating.

    Premium detailing

    A complete, unhurried restoration of a vehicle’s presentation, inside and out. The order matters more than any single product.

    Inspect Walk the car under a controlled light and record what is really there.
    Decontaminate Wash and remove bonded contamination so nothing is dragged across the paint later.
    Correct Only where the paint needs it, and only as far as it needs.
    Protect Seal the finished surface with the protection that suits the car and its owner.
    Review A final inspection, in light, with the owner.
